Per-route offsets into the operator bank live on the OPERATORS tab; these slots are everything else.
Mockup only. No engine, no params — every control holds its own value. Values read out in a popup on hover and during a drag, never printed on the faceplate. Drag faders and dials vertically, route constants horizontally; drag the EG breakpoints and the key-scaling handles directly on their graphs; click glyphs on an LFO's wave knob, cells in the PM grid, and the operator tabs. The operator waveform is a dropdown with a drawn preview — eleven waveforms is past what a rotary selector can carry. Switching preset relabels and rescales the eight macros, which is the point of the macro row: the labels are patch data, the knob indices are not.
